              ## Who We Are
            _template: richtext
      - content:
          - text: |
              The Out(sider) Preservation Initiative (OPI) commemorates Black place preservation and persistence across places and time, focusing on the creation of Black settlements and towns. OPI activities, research and grantmaking highlight the roles culture, performance, storytelling and heritage play in descendants’ approaches to preserving, revitalizing, remembering and protecting freedmen’s settlements, also known as Freedom Colonies, Black Settlements, Black Colonies, Black Towns and Freedom Communities.

              This Mellon-funded initiative expands the mapping, technical assistance and storytelling work of The Texas Freedom Colonies Project. OPI’s home is the Center for Cultural Landscapes (CCL) at the UVA School of Architecture.
